# My skills, by section

Your skills live in 5 plugins, one folder each here. In Claude they show as `plugin:skill`,
for example `/instagram:ig-reel`. Or just describe what you want and Claude picks the right one.

Sections 1–2 are the `website-design` plugin, 3 is `website-checks`, 4–6 are `instagram`,
7 is `marketing-brand` and 8 is `writing`.

## 1. Website design and look
| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| frontend-design-direction | Picks a clear design style for a site or page so it doesn't look generic |
| make-interfaces-feel-better | Small polish details: spacing, text, shadows, buttons, hover and tap states |
| apple-look | Reviews a design against Apple's design rules (apps, screens, mockups) |
| design-system | Pulls out a site's colors, fonts and spacing into one set of rules, and checks they're used consistently |
| mobile-native | Makes the site feel smooth on phones: no stuck highlights after a tap, no zoom when typing, nothing cut off by the notch |
| moodboard | Shows 2 or 3 looks side by side (colors, fonts, photos, sample buttons) so you can pick one before building |
| page-builder | Turns an idea for a page into 2 quick mockups, then builds the real page and checks it on desktop and phone |

## 2. Animation and motion
| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| apple-motion | Smooth, natural-feeling animations in Apple's style |
| improve-animations | Reviews all the animations on a site and plans fixes |
| review-animations | Strict check of animation code. Only runs when you type `/review-animations` |

## 3. Website checks
| Skill | What it does |

## 4. Instagram: make posts
| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| ig-reel | Writes a reel: the hook, the script and the on-screen text |
| ig-carousel | Builds a swipe post, slide by slide, with the image files |
| ig-story | Plans the day's stories and which stickers to use |
| ig-caption | Writes the caption and picks the hashtags |
| ig-human | Makes any text sound like a person wrote it, not AI |

## 5. Instagram: grow and talk to people
| Skill | What it does |

| ig-comment | Writes comments to leave on other people's posts |
| ig-reply | Replies to comments on your own posts |
| ig-dm | Writes DMs: first messages, collab pitches, follow-ups |

## 6. Instagram: plan and learn
| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|

## 7. Marketing and brand
| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| brand-discovery | A step-by-step interview to figure out your brand (who it's for, how it sounds) |
| brand-kit | Reads a website and writes down its colors, fonts, tone and products in one file, then makes on-brand posts |
| brand-voice | Learns how you write from your real posts, so new writing sounds like you |
| marketing-campaign | Plans a full launch: ads, emails, social posts, landing page |
| market-research | Researches competitors and the market |
